Credit Card Risk Mart

30,000 card accounts · monthly snapshots Apr–Sep 2005 · default observed Oct 2005 · amounts in NT$

Source: UCI Default of Credit Card Clients (Yeh & Lien, 2009), built into a DuckDB mart by build_mart.py. Buckets come from the repayment status code: ≤0 Current, 1 = 30, 2 = 60, ≥3 = 90+. Exposure is the positive statement balance. Segment filters are for monitoring; sex, age and marital status are not inputs to credit decisions.
